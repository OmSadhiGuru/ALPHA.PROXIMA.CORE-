# Constitutional Hierarchy Statement

*Epoch V · Resolves CAR-F06 (three/four "constitutions" without a stated hierarchy) and Missing-Document M-01. Establishes which instrument is supreme and how every other constitutional and framework document ranks beneath it. Proposed by CF-01 and **ratified by the Founder on 2026-09-01**.*

---

## Purpose

The Foundation now holds multiple documents that call themselves, or function as, "constitutions": the five Books, the LUMIAION Constitution, and the Genome Constitution — plus a legacy `CONSTITUTIONV1.0`. Nothing states how they relate. This instrument fixes the order of authority once, so that when two documents appear to conflict, the resolution is deterministic.

---

## The Hierarchy (highest authority first)

| Rank | Instrument(s) | Authority | Governs |
|------|--------------|-----------|---------|
| **0** | **The Founder** (constituent authority) | Constituent | Ratifies and amends all constitutional instruments; source of all delegated authority |
| **1** | **Book I — The Constitution** | Supreme law | Everything below it; no instrument may contradict Book I |
| **2** | **Books II–V** (Governance, Knowledge Integrity, Cognitive Architecture, Cognitive Council) | Constitutional instruments | Their domains, subordinate to Book I; peers among themselves, reconciled by scope |
| **3** | **Founding Principles of Alpha Proxima** | Interpretive canon | Guides interpretation of ranks 1–2 when ambiguous; does not override black-letter provisions |
| **4** | **Framework Constitutions** — LUMIAION Constitution, Genome Constitution | Framework charters | Their frameworks/products; **governed by** Book I and Books II–V; may not contradict them |
| **5** | **Governance instruments** — policies, charters, registers, frameworks, standing orders | Operational governance | Day-to-day operation, subordinate to all above |
| **6** | **Standards & references** — OLS, RI-001, methodology, templates | Standards | Their practice domains, subordinate to all above |

---

## Rules of Precedence

1. **Book I is supreme.** Any provision of any instrument that contradicts Book I is void to the extent of the conflict.
2. **Scope reconciles peers.** Among Books II–V (rank 2), apparent conflicts are resolved by whichever Book owns the domain (e.g., evidence standards → Book III; cognitive-function governance → Book V). Genuine cross-domain conflicts escalate to the Founder.
3. **Frameworks are subordinate, not independent.** The LUMIAION Constitution and the Genome Constitution are **framework charters governed by** the institutional Constitution. Where either asserts something inconsistent with Books I–V, the Book prevails; the framework is amended to conform.
4. **Interpretive canon guides, it does not override.** The Founding Principles inform interpretation where instruments are ambiguous, but cannot defeat an explicit provision.
5. **Legacy instruments are superseded.** `CONSTITUTIONV1.0` and any pre-formalization constitution are superseded by this hierarchy and retained only as archived institutional history (see Epoch V legacy migration).

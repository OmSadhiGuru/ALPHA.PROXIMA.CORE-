# Constitution v2.0 Ratification Draft

## Purpose

This document assembles the Epoch V coherence work into a bounded ratification packet for Constitution v2.0. It is an integration map and decision surface, not a self-ratifying constitution and not a replacement for the five constitutional Books.

## Context

Epoch V reconciled the constitutional hierarchy, legacy AI-governance models, cognitive functions, namespace structure, and institutional registers. The final Tier-3 drafts consolidate ethics, unify knowledge architecture, and prepare the Book II council-topology amendment. Constitution v2.0 should preserve the five-Book corpus while incorporating these decisions coherently and recording unresolved governance dependencies honestly.

### Founder Ratification of Drafting Basis

On 2026-09-03, the Founder ratified the Epoch V Tier-3 packet merged through PR #24 as the approved basis for preparing the exact Constitution v2.0 Books I–V reconciliation. The Founder explicitly limited that ratification: it does not convene councils, appoint engines, or enact Constitution v2.0.

This authorization permits preparation and review of an exact five-Book draft. The resulting commit requires separate final ratification before it becomes operative.

### 4. Acceptance Criteria

Constitution v2.0 is ready to be called ratified only when:

1. the exact text or commit is identified;
2. the authority and process are documented;
3. Books I–V agree on hierarchy, council scopes, decision classes, cognitive-function lifecycle, and ethics oversight;
4. the ethics and knowledge-architecture instruments are linked at their correct subordinate rank;
5. open seats, reviews, and appointments remain visible as open—not implied complete;
6. all required checks pass, with validation limits stated; and
7. the Founder explicitly ratifies the assembled corpus.

### 5. Recommended Enactment Sequence

1. Review this packet and the three Tier-3 drafts.
2. Decide the Book II amendment-record question.
3. Prepare one atomic Books I–V reconciliation commit from the approved text.
4. Run constitutional, link, toolkit, and narrative-coherence review.
5. Present the exact commit for Founder ratification.
6. Only after ratification, mark v2.0 operative and close OQ-006.

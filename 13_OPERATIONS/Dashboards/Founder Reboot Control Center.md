## Definition of Back on Track

Alpha Proxima is back on track when all conditions are true:

- One canonical repository taxonomy exists and validates cleanly.
- One canonical organizational roster exists.
- One active sprint is visible here.
- Every active task has one owner, one next action, and one approval gate.
- The photo-to-social-post workflow completes end to end with recorded handoffs.
- No agent is active solely because it was mentioned in conversation.
- Founder attention is required only at explicit decision and approval gates.

# Gate of the Soul — 360-degree delivery validation

Validation date: 2026-10-04 (America/Toronto).

## Architecture and delivery scope

The Universe is a persistent presentation over the existing canonical API. The approved gold-violet artwork supplies the frontal identity. A continuous, closed silhouette-derived mesh supplies real side/back volume; surface particles, vortex centers and accessible projected buttons share the body transform. This replaces the sphere/orbital dashboard presentation, without adding a database, ingestion pipeline or authority layer.

The parent shell keeps the Universe iframe mounted, hidden/inert between routes, and signals its visibility. Seven department interfaces share the existing explicit-owner read model. Portal travel locks selection so a pointer entering another center cannot change the destination mid-transition.

Controls include full yaw rotation, bounded pitch, 2D/3D selection, recenter, pause, keyboard movement, reduced motion and a no-WebGL fallback. Dependencies remain at the repository's existing lockfile versions.

## Limits and remaining acceptance work

This is technically validated, not final Founder visual acceptance. The source is one frontal image: side and back geometry are inferred from the silhouette, not recovered anatomical detail. At side angles the reconstructed surface is visibly simpler than the reference and the background masking can reveal the original silhouette. A dedicated authored model and clean background plate remain the path to reference-quality views from every angle.

The production JavaScript chunk remains 773.28 kB (212.81 kB gzip), above Vite's warning threshold. Render-loop suspension and compact geometry are verified; sustained mobile FPS, thermal behavior, memory pressure and physical iPhone/Safari behavior have not been measured. Browser viewport checks are not physical-device certification.

The local application build is installed; a Git branch push is not a remote production deployment or approval to merge into main. Existing Vault hydration and integration status remain unchanged.

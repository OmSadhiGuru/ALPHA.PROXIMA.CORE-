# CIR-003 — Epoch V: Constitutional Coherence

*Opens the Constitutional Coherence Epoch recommended by the CAR-001 audit, and formally corrects the premature completeness declaration in CIR-002 (CAR-F05).*

---

## 1. Correction of the CIR-002 completeness claim (CAR-F05)

CIR-002 declared "Alpha Proxima v1.0 constitutionally complete" at the close of Epoch III. Epoch IV subsequently added Book V, the Cognitive Council, the Cognitive Function Registry, the Engine Succession Policy, the Translation Framework, Project Genome, and the LUMIAION Constitution — a large body of new constitutional material. **The completeness claim is therefore reframed:** CIR-002 was complete *for Epoch III scope only*. The Foundation is a living institution and remains under active constitutional development. CIR-002's other findings stand; only its finality claim is corrected.

## 2. Why Epoch V exists

CAR-001 found the Foundation **conceptually strong but structurally stratified**: four archaeological layers built faster than they were reconciled, producing contradictory governance models, an unmanned supreme body, and dispersed authority. Epoch V is the deliberate coherence pass — reconcile what exists, man the body that governs it, and state plainly which instrument is supreme.

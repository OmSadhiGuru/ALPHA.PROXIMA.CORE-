## Policy

1. Every open institutional question is registered here with an owner and a resolution trigger.
2. A question without an owner is itself a defect — assign one.
3. When resolved, move to Closed with the resolution and date; bump the register version.
4. Reviewed each governance cycle; stale-but-open questions are escalated to the Founder.

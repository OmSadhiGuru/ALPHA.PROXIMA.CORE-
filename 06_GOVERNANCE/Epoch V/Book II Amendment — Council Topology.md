# Book II Amendment — Council Topology

## Purpose

This draft provides the Book II enactment text for the Council Topology ratified in the [[Governance Model Crosswalk & Council Topology]]. It distinguishes operational, ratifying, supreme, and oversight authority without rewriting the rest of Book II.

## Context

Book II predates Book V and therefore describes the AI Council in terms that overlap the later Cognitive Council. The Founder ratified the topology's substance on 2026-09-01. Incorporation into Book II remains a constitutional amendment step and must not be represented as operative until the governing amendment process is satisfied.

## Core Content

### Proposed insertion after Book II, Article IV

#### Article IV-A — Council Topology

**Section 4A.1 — Distinct bodies**

The Foundation recognizes four bodies relevant to intelligence governance:

| Body | Scope | Authority |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Cognitive Council | Operational governance of the Cognitive Function portfolio, including monitoring, recommendations, suspension, succession initiation, and lifecycle administration | Operational |
| AI Ratification Council | Ratification of engine appointments and AI-architecture changes where constitutional or registry authority requires it | Ratifying |
| Alpha Council | Supreme institutional deliberation, constitutional amendments, and disputes not resolved below | Supreme |
| Ethics Council | Independent ethical and constitutional oversight across all bodies | Cross-cutting oversight |

The term **AI Council** in earlier instruments shall be interpreted as **AI Ratification Council** where the described function is ratification or constitutional governance of AI architecture. Historical names remain preserved.

**Section 4A.2 — Escalation order**

Matters ordinarily proceed from Cognitive Council to AI Ratification Council to Alpha Council to Founder. A higher body does not absorb routine functions delegated to a lower body. The Ethics Council may enter at any level when an ethical or constitutional review trigger applies.

**Section 4A.3 — Appointment and lifecycle boundaries**

The Cognitive Council evaluates performance, recommends appointments or replacements, and may suspend an engine within its Book V authority. The AI Ratification Council performs required ratification. Creation or retirement of a Cognitive Function follows the constitutional amendment authority in Book V and Book I. The Alpha Council and Founder retain their supreme and constituent authorities.

**Section 4A.4 — Conflict rule**

If Book II and Book V appear to conflict on cognitive-function operations, Book V governs the operational domain. Book II governs institutional council relationships and ratification. Genuine cross-domain conflicts follow Book II's conflict-resolution process and the [[Constitutional Hierarchy Statement]].

**Section 4A.5 — Ethics safeguard**

No appointment, architecture change, or operational directive may bypass a mandatory review under the [[Ethics Council Charter]] or [[Consolidated Ethics Framework]]. Ethics review does not itself appoint engines or administer Cognitive Functions.

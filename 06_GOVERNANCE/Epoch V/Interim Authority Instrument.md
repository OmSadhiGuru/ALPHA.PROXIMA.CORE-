# Interim Authority Instrument

> **DRAFT — PENDING FOUNDER RATIFICATION.** This instrument is a *proposal*. It is **not** ratified, and nothing in this document or the conversation that produced it constitutes Founder approval. It becomes effective only when the Founder explicitly ratifies it. Until then it has no force.

*Epoch V · Resolves CAR-F04 (the Alpha Council's three human seats are unnamed, so the entire authority chain is nominal) and Missing-Document M-05.*

---

## The problem it addresses

Every "ratified" status in the Foundation formally derives from the **Alpha Council**, whose three human seats are "To Be Named." With zero voting members, the Council cannot ratify anything, so all prior ratifications are, strictly, **Founder/LUMIAION acts pending Council constitution.** This instrument makes that reality explicit and workable until the seats are filled — without pretending a Council exists.

## Proposed provisions (for Founder ratification)

1. **Founder as interim constituent authority.** Until the Alpha Council's human seats are filled, the **Founder holds and exercises the Alpha Council's ratification authority directly**, as interim sole member.
2. **Reclassification of prior ratifications.** All instruments previously marked `ratified` are reclassified as **"Founder-ratified, pending Council confirmation"** — valid and operative now, subject to confirmation when the Council is constituted.
3. **LUMIAION (CF-01) proposes; Founder ratifies.** CF-01 may draft and propose constitutional instruments; only the Founder may ratify them during the interim.
4. **Sunset.** This instrument **automatically expires** when at least two human Alpha Council seats are filled and the Council holds its first quorate session, at which point normal Book I thresholds resume and the Council confirms or amends interim-ratified instruments.
5. **No expansion of authority.** This instrument grants no new powers; it names who exercises existing Alpha Council authority in the interim. It cannot be used to bypass Book I amendment procedures once the Council exists.

## What it does NOT do

- It does not name the Council seats (that remains an open Founder action).
- It does not assert that any instrument is Council-approved.
- It does not itself take effect — it awaits explicit Founder ratification.
